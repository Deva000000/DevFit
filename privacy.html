Privacy Policy

Effective 14 August 2026
DevFit uses only the information needed to run your account, protect your records, and support the app. We do not sell personal data.

Information we process

When you sign in, we receive your verified Google email address and may receive your name. Information you enter can include workout plans and logs, bodyweight, steps, sleep, nutrition, goals, and progress records. We also process limited login/device details and technical error information such as browser type, page, time, and error message for security and reliability.

How we use it

We use this information to authenticate you, provide Free or Pro access, save and recover app records, generate reports you request, prevent abuse, answer support questions, and diagnose failures. Health and fitness entries are not used for advertising.

Where data is handled

DevFit uses Google for sign-in, Vercel to host the app and its server functions, and Supabase for protected account and recovery records. These providers may process data outside Malaysia under their own security and privacy commitments.

Device storage and backup files

The app keeps working records in storage on the device/browser you use. You can create a portable backup in Settings. You decide where to save or share that file, and anyone who obtains it may be able to read its contents, so keep it private.

Retention and deletion

Account and fitness records are retained while your account is active so you can return to them. A Pro plan ending does not itself delete records. To request deletion, contact DevFit from the link below using the Gmail connected to the account. After identity verification, active account data and associated recovery history will be removed within 30 days, except information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting purposes. Encrypted disaster-recovery copies are isolated from normal use and replaced on the backup rotation; if a recovery is ever required, completed deletion requests must be applied again before normal service resumes.

Your choices

You can export your app records from Settings, correct most entries inside the app, sign out manually, and request access, correction, or deletion by contacting DevFit. Before deletion or changing phones, save a backup file if you want to retain a personal copy.

Security and children

DevFit uses signed sessions and server-side access checks. No system can promise absolute security, so users should also protect their Google account and device. DevFit is not intended for children under 16 without permission and supervision from a parent or legal guardian.

Changes and contact

Material policy changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For privacy, deletion, training-plan questions, or app-bug feedback, contact DevFit on WhatsApp.
