How DevFit works
New here? Do these three things
1
Set your target. Open Progress → Program setup and enter your start weight, goal weight and how many weeks you're giving it. Everything else in the app measures against this.
2
Build one workout. Go to Training → Edit Plan, add a routine (say "Push"), and add a few exercises with target sets × reps. You only have to do this once.
3
Log today. Enter this morning's bodyweight on Progress, tick off your sets under Training → Log Session, and add your meals in Nutrition. That's the daily habit — about two minutes.
Log for one week and the app starts telling you things you can't see day to day — whether your weight is genuinely moving, whether your lifts are climbing, and whether you're eating enough to recover.
What each tab does
◉
Progress — your home tabLog bodyweight, steps and sleep each day. Today Overview shows them against your goals. Training Status reads last night's sleep and your recent workload to tell you whether you're recovered enough to train hard today, and which routine is due next. The Daily / Weekly / Analytics tabs at the top switch between logging today, reviewing the week, and seeing your trend.
▦
Training — plan it, then log itTwo modes. Edit Plan is where you build routines and add moves — strength (sets × reps) or cardio (duration, distance, and sport-specific stats like rounds or goals). Log Session is where you fill in what you actually did. The Last column beside each set shows what you hit last time, so you always know what to beat, and a pill under each exercise tells you if your total volume went up, down or stayed flat. Save routines you reuse with plan libraries, and export the whole program as a PDF.
🍎
Nutrition — targets, then a food diaryStart with the macro calculator: it works out your maintenance calories (Mifflin-St Jeor, or Katch-McArdle if you know your body fat) and multiplies by your activity level, then splits the result into protein, carbs and fat for your goal. Then log meals against those targets in the daily diary — search by name, or scan a barcode with your camera. The ring at the top shows how much of the day you have left.
★
Score & Analytics (Pro)Your weekly True Progress Score blends seven logged signals — bodyweight direction, optional body-fat direction, training overload, diet adherence, sleep, steps and stress — using DevFit's clearly shown product weighting. The headline score appears only when at least three signals provide enough coverage. Charts plot your bodyweight and score over time, and you can export a weekly or full-program PDF report.
⤬
Share cardsTurn a session into a clean branded image you can post. Tap Share today's win at the bottom of Log Session for a workout or cardio card, or share a weekly progress card from Progress. Light, dark and transparent themes. Set your @handle and card font under Personal → Share Card below.
↓
Your data & backupYour entries save on this device as you log them. Under Data & Account, use Save / Share Backup File regularly and keep the file in Files, Google Drive, iCloud Drive, or another safe place you control.
⚙
Make it feel like an appUnder App below you can install DevFit to your home screen (it then runs full-screen and works offline), switch between light and dark themes, and turn on an evening reminder to log your day.
✦
Free vs ProFree lets you log everything, every day, forever — it just shows you the current week. Pro (RM19.90/month) opens your full history, the True Progress Score, trend charts, per-exercise progression and PDF reports. Nothing you log is ever deleted: if a plan lapses, your history is kept safe and reappears the moment you renew.

Install DevFit
Choose your phone below and follow the exact steps.
Android · Google Chrome
Chrome can install DevFit directly as a full-screen app.
  1. Open devfitportal.vercel.app in Chrome.
  2. If the red install button appears above, tap it. Otherwise tap Chrome’s ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Tap Install to confirm, then open DevFit from your home screen.
iPhone / iPad · Safari
Apple allows home-screen installation only from Safari. Chrome, Firefox and Edge on iPhone cannot install it.
  1. Open devfitportal.vercel.app in Safari.
  2. Tap Safari’s Share icon—the square with the upward arrow.
  3. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Keep the name DevFit, then tap Add in the top-right.
  5. Open the DevFit icon from your home screen and sign in once.
